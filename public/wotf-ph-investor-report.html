Methodology

How We Project 2,500 Foot Traffic

Our 2,500 foot traffic projection is grounded in observable registration data, combined with industry-validated multipliers from Philippine-based Taekwondo and combat sports events.

Tournament Athlete Baseline
350 athletes are currently registered across Kyorugi, Poomsae, and Kyukpa disciplines. With registration still open through the April 10 deadline, we project a final count of 370–390 competing athletes — already a historically strong field for a regional event.
Family & Supporter Multiplier
Philippine martial arts events consistently draw 2.5–3.5 family members or supporters per competing athlete. Applying a conservative 2.8× multiplier on tournament athletes alone generates over 1,000 additional non-athlete visitors to the venue.
Officials, Staff & Media
A 3-discipline national championship requires substantial operational personnel. We account for approximately 80 accredited referees and judges, 120 club officials and coaches, and 50+ media and sponsor representatives on-site across the event.
Promotion Test & Seminar Audience
Day 2 includes the Promotion Test and the Olympic Sparring Camp led by Olympic Gold Medalist Master Moon Dae Sung — a high-profile draw that will attract additional spectators, parents, and martial arts communities beyond the tournament registrant pool.
Multi-Day Attendance Pattern
Athletes competing on multiple days, coaches attending all sessions, and returning spectators naturally generate repeat visits. We conservatively estimate a 15% repeat-visit uplift across three days — approximately 175–200 additional headcount entries.
Digital Platform & Walk-In Outreach
WOTF PH's digital presence through wotf-ph.com and our 426-user platform community drives organic awareness. We project 300–400 walk-in spectators over the three days — a conservative estimate for a nationally recognized federation event held in Davao's active martial arts community.
