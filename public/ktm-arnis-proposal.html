Core System Capabilities

Tournament Management

Create multi-discipline events with automated bracket generation, category seeding, registration windows, and entry fee configuration for Anyo and Laban.

Online Athlete Registration

Arnisadors register and pay online. Admins approve, reject, or request corrections from a single dashboard — no spreadsheets needed.

QR Code Check-In

Each athlete receives a unique QR card. Staff scan via USB scanner, webcam, or manual search — with real-time attendance tracking.

Secure Online Payments

Registration fees collected upfront through secure checkout. Per-athlete payment tracking with support for multiple payment channels.

Digital Waiver Signing

Athletes sign waivers digitally at check-in via a dual-screen display. PDFs auto-generated and saved per event — fully paperless.

Financial Dashboard

Live revenue tracking across all event types. Track expected vs. collected amounts, view breakdowns, and export reports.

Organization & Club Management

Club Master Accounts

Each club gets a dashboard to manage rosters, submit team registrations, track payment status, and view competition history.

Athlete ID & QR Cards

Unique 5-digit Player IDs and downloadable QR cards sent automatically via email upon registration approval.
