Notes on Figures
📋 Variance & Validation Notes
  • Primary source: All figures are taken directly from the KTM Sports Technology app registration database. No manual encoding or external spreadsheet data has been used.
  • No variance to note: The app registration count is considered the single validated source. Any walk-in or manually enrolled participants not captured in the app are not included in this official figure.
  • Data cutoff: Figures reflect the registration status as of the date of this document. Registration periods for all three events are considered closed.
  • Official use: This document is intended as the single, final, and defensible count for all Tomatok platform reporting and Chairman-level communication.
